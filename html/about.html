About Our Company

Travel Explorer is a travel recommendation platform that helps people discover beautiful destinations around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make travel planning easier by providing information about beaches, temples, countries, and interesting places.

Our Team

Our team consists of students interested in web development, programming, and tourism. We work together to create a useful travel website.

Team Members and Responsibilities

  • Team Leader — Project organization and coordination.
  • Web Developer — HTML and CSS development.
  • Content Manager — Destination information and descriptions.

Guidance for Team Members

  • Complete assigned tasks on time.
  • Communicate with other team members.
  • Write clean and organized code.
  • Test all website pages before submission.
  • Respect team members and share ideas.

Our Values

Creativity, teamwork, responsibility, communication, and continuous learning.
